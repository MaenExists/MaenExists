MaenExists is a personal blog and open source Astro setup focused on fast publishing, plain Markdown, and a minimal reading experience that feels closer to a working environment than a marketing page.

## What this is

A personal blog for build logs, interface decisions, and the kind of technical notes that are still useful months later.

## Design rules

- Dark mode by default
- One accent color
- Flat surfaces, no depth tricks
- Spacing over decoration

## Publishing stack

Astro, Markdown, plain CSS, and Cloudflare Pages. Small enough to stay fast, stable, and easy to maintain.

## Why it exists

The goal is to make publishing frictionless and reading calm. Less branding, more signal.
