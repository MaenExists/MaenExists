# About Maen

Hi, I'm Maen (Mayan). I'm a high school student from India with a broke potato computer but big dreams. I love building real stuff, tinkering with AI, and creating things that actually matter.

This blog is where I share my journey - the wins, the failures, and everything in between. Expect build logs, technical deep dives, and honest reflections on what it's like to learn and create as a lone wolf tinkerer.

## What you'll find here

- **Build logs**: Detailed walkthroughs of projects I'm working on
- **Tech notes**: Things I learn while tinkering with AI, web dev, and low-spec optimization
- **Honest reflections**: The good, the bad, and the ugly of self-taught building
- **Open source projects**: Code I'm sharing with the world

## Why this exists

School hasn't been my strong suit, but I know my stuff when it comes to building. This is my space to document my growth from broke tinkerer to someone who can actually ship real tech that advances humanity.

Stay tuned for the grind, the breakthroughs, and everything in between.
